# Health Lake SAR Ingestion & Record Extraction

## Strategic Context
Manual patient record collation across fragmented electronic health record systems takes hours per Subject Access Request (SAR), exposing practices to missed 30-day statutory deadlines under UK GDPR. Automating the querying and ingestion of longitudinal medical records from the OneAdvanced Health Lake warehouse provides a normalized, vendor-agnostic foundation for rapid extraction across EMIS Web, TPP SystmOne, and Vision without bespoke connector maintenance.

## Success Criteria
- Complete patient record retrieval across consultations, medications, investigations, and attachments within 15 seconds of request trigger.
- 100% data normalization into structured JSON/FHIR models matching UK clinical safety standards (DCB0129).

## Scope

### In Scope
- Asynchronous querying of OneAdvanced Health Lake via secure mTLS REST/FHIR endpoints.
- Extraction of longitudinal clinical events: coded consultations, encounter notes, lab results, immunisations, and document attachments.
- Date range filtering and encounter boundary segmentation for targeted SAR extraction.
- Ephemeral caching of ingested clinical payload in encrypted Redis with automatic 4-hour TTL expiry.

### Out of Scope
- Direct legacy database queries to on-premise EMIS or SystmOne servers (defer to OneAdvanced Health Lake normalization layer).
- Patient identity matching across distinct practice registries (handled via NHS Number and CIS2 authentication).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a practice user initiates a SAR extraction for a verified NHS number, the Health Lake Ingestion Gateway SHALL asynchronously query the OneAdvanced Health Lake and stage normalized clinical records within 15 seconds.

WHERE date range parameters are provided, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL filter encounters, coded entries, and attachments to the specified temporal boundaries.

IF OneAdvanced Health Lake returns a timeout or connection failure, THEN the Ingestion Gateway SHALL retry up to 3 times with exponential backoff before logging an audit alert and notifying the user.

WHEN raw clinical records are staged for processing, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL write payload data exclusively to ephemeral encrypted cache with a 4-hour time-to-live.

## Business Rules
- All requests must include a valid 10-digit NHS Number verified against PDS standards.
- Ingested clinical payloads must never be persisted to unencrypted disk or relational storage outside ephemeral review cache.
- System must comply with NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT) requirements.

## Dependencies
- OneAdvanced Health Lake REST/FHIR API availability and mutual TLS certificate provisioning.
- Redis ElastiCache cluster with encryption at rest and transit.
