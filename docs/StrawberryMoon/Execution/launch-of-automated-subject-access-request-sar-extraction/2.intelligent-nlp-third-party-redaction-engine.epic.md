# Intelligent NLP Third-Party Redaction Engine

## Strategic Context
Accidental disclosure of third-party identifiable data (such as family members, non-treating acquaintances, and safeguarding markers) represents the primary regulatory risk under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 during SAR disclosure. Implementing an automated, context-aware clinical NLP redaction engine replaces unreliable manual marker-pen redaction with high-precision automated entity masking, safeguarding practice compliance while cutting review overhead by over 90%.

## Success Criteria
- Greater than 98% precision and recall in detecting third-party identifiable references and safeguarding markers across clinical narratives.
- Sub-5-second NLP processing latency per 100 clinical encounter notes.

## Scope

### In Scope
- BioBERT and UK Clinical NLP transformer models containerized on AWS ECS Fargate.
- Automated entity recognition for third-party names, relations, contact details, and sensitive safeguarding codes.
- Confidence scoring and character offset bounding generation for detected third-party entities.
- Redaction classification tagging (Third-Party Family, Non-Clinical Personnel, Safeguarding Marker, Irrelevant Health Data).

### Out of Scope
- Automated redaction of scanned handwritten historical attachments without OCR text layer (defer to manual review workspace).
- Permanent deletion of clinical source data within the upstream EHR repository.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN raw clinical narrative text is received by the NLP Redaction Engine, the NLP Service SHALL scan the text and generate entity bounding tokens with confidence scores within 5 seconds per 100 encounters.

WHERE a detected entity has a confidence score equal to or exceeding 0.85, the NLP Service SHALL apply a default pre-selected redaction mask.

IF a text segment contains high-risk safeguarding terminology, THEN the NLP Service SHALL flag the record with a mandatory clinical review warning badge.

WHEN redaction boundaries are calculated, the NLP Service SHALL emit structured JSON containing character start/end offsets, entity classification, and rule identifiers.

## Business Rules
- High-risk safeguarding markers must never be automatically dismissed without explicit human clinician or administrator interaction.
- Redaction offsets must align precisely with the underlying UTF-8 character indices of the normalized EHR payload.
- Zero patient identifiable data or clinical text may be written to NLP model inference log files.

## Dependencies
- Pre-trained and evaluated UK Clinical Transformer model artifacts stored in UK-sovereign S3 model registry.
- Containerized inference service running on AWS ECS Fargate with GPU acceleration where required.
