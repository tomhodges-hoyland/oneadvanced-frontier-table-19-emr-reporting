# Federated administrative hub enabling Primary Care Networks

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** EMR Reporting
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
EMR Reporting Launches Federated Administrative Hub for Primary Care Networks to Streamline Cross-Practice Reporting and Subject Access Requests

## Summary
EMR Reporting introduces a federated administrative hub that enables Primary Care Networks (PCNs) to centralize reporting workloads, route Subject Access Requests (SARs) across member practices, and standardize administrative operations. This unified platform eliminates siloed workflows, reduces administrative burden, and improves compliance across networked healthcare providers.

## Problem Statement
Primary Care Networks operate across multiple independent practices, each managing their own administrative workflows, reporting processes, and regulatory requests. This fragmentation creates inefficiency: SARs must be manually routed between practices, reporting standards vary across the network, and administrative teams lack visibility into cross-practice workloads. The result is delayed responses, compliance risk, and wasted operational effort duplicating similar tasks across member organizations.

## Solution
EMR Reporting's federated administrative hub provides a single, centralized point of control for PCN-wide administrative operations. The platform enables network administrators to route SARs intelligently to the appropriate member practice, consolidate reporting across the entire network, and enforce standardized processes while respecting the autonomy of individual practices. Practices remain operationally independent but benefit from shared administrative infrastructure and coordinated workflows.
