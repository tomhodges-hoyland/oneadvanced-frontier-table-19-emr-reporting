# EHR Ingestion & SNOMED-CT Chronology Mapping

## Strategic Context
General practice staff spend 4 to 8 hours weekly manually reviewing disparate primary care record systems to extract relevant patient histories for statutory and commercial reports. Ingesting longitudinal clinical data through the OneAdvanced Health Lake warehouse decouples reporting workflows from vendor-specific EHR schemas (EMIS Web, TPP SystmOne, Vision) and provides a unified, normalized data stream. Establishing automated SNOMED-CT timeline extraction eliminates manual search overhead, accelerates report generation from weeks to minutes, and ensures clinical accuracy.

## Success Criteria
- Complete FHIR/REST clinical extraction from OneAdvanced Health Lake in under 15 seconds per patient query.
- 100% normalization of primary care encounters, consultations, problem lists, lab results, and medication histories into standardized SNOMED-CT chronological entities.
- Zero persistent raw EHR data retained beyond active ephemeral session TTL.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Mutual TLS integration with OneAdvanced Health Lake REST/FHIR endpoints.
- Normalized parsing of clinical encounters, consultations, diagnoses, laboratory investigations, and medication histories.
- Chronological timeline construction with practitioner attribution and SNOMED-CT coding tags.
- In-memory ephemeral buffering and Redis session caching with strict TTL expiry.

### Out of Scope
- Direct direct-database connections to on-premise GP practice servers (abstracted via Health Lake).
- Modification or write-back of clinical notes into primary care EHR systems.
- Non-primary care acute hospital EPR record ingestion.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a medical secretary initiates an extraction request with a valid NHS Number, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL retrieve all corresponding encounters, lab results, problems, and medication records from OneAdvanced Health Lake via mutual TLS within 15 seconds.

WHEN raw clinical records are retrieved from Health Lake, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL normalize coded entries into chronological SNOMED-CT timeline items ordered by encounter date.

WHERE consultation entries include free-text narratives, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL preserve clinician attribution, consultation date, and original coding hierarchy in the normalized timeline object.

IF a Health Lake query times out or returns an authentication error, the Ingestion Gateway SHALL log an immutable audit event without patient identifiable data and emit a retry alert to the worklist queue.

## Business Rules
- Raw clinical extraction data buffered in Redis must have a strict Time-To-Live (TTL) of 4 hours, after which all uncommitted in-memory records are purged.
- Ingestion queries must filter records strictly based on the authorized scope and reporting time window of the specific request.
- All outbound queries to OneAdvanced Health Lake must use UK sovereign VPC routing and TLS 1.3 encryption.

## Dependencies
- OneAdvanced Health Lake FHIR/REST partner API connectivity.
- UK sovereign AWS eu-west-2 infrastructure with KMS customer-managed key encryption.
- Primary Care NHS CIS2 authentication service.
