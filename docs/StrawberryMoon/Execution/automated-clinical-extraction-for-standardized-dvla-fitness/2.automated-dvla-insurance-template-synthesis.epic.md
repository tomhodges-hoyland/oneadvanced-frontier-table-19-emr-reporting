# Automated DVLA & Insurance Template Synthesis

## Strategic Context
Primary care medical reporting requires translating dense medical chronologies into rigid external questionnaires, such as DVLA fitness-to-drive forms, ABI insurance reports, and DWP medical assessments. Administrative staff manually transcribing answers face high cognitive load, risking omitted diagnoses or misclassified medical timelines. Automating questionnaire synthesis maps extracted SNOMED-CT clinical codes directly into target questionnaire schemas, standardizing responses and cutting administrative assembly time to seconds while maintaining regulatory compliance.

## Success Criteria
- 100% automated field mapping accuracy against official DVLA (e.g. D4, medical fitness forms) and ABI standardized schemas.
- Generation of populated questionnaire drafts within 5 seconds of clinical chronology availability.
- Support for customizable rich-text inserts in bespoke legal letter formats.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Schema mapping engine for standardized DVLA fitness-to-drive forms (cardiovascular, diabetes, visual, neurological).
- Template synthesis for ABI life/critical illness insurance questionnaires and DWP assessment forms.
- Dynamic data binding linking synthesized form fields to underlying SNOMED-CT timeline source snippets.
- PDF/A compliant document compilation and structured digital export.

### Out of Scope
- Automated medical eligibility determination or autonomous clinical fitness-to-drive declarations without GP sign-off.
- Direct submission API integration to DVLA/ABI endpoints (delivered via secure gated download portal).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN normalized SNOMED-CT timeline data is ingested for a DVLA request, the Synthesis Engine SHALL auto-populate all matching questionnaire fields with relevant clinical events, diagnosis dates, and medication dosages.

WHEN a report field is populated, the Synthesis Engine SHALL attach a bidirectional reference pointer linking the field directly to its source EHR timeline snippet.

WHERE an inquiry requires bespoke free-text reporting, the Synthesis Engine SHALL render a markdown-based letter builder with click-to-insert clinical timeline entries.

IF required medical history for a mandatory questionnaire question is absent from the ingested EHR dataset, the Synthesis Engine SHALL flag the field with an "Unresolved Evidence" indicator for manual staff review.

## Business Rules
- DVLA and insurance form templates must be version-controlled, preventing the use of deprecated regulatory questionnaire revisions.
- Every auto-populated response must maintain persistent linkage to the original encounter date and clinician attribution.
- No generated questionnaire draft may be compiled for external distribution without passing validation on all mandatory fields.

## Dependencies
- Normalized clinical timeline objects from the EHR Ingestion & SNOMED-CT Chronology Mapping module.
- Template registry containing validated DVLA, ABI, and DWP form schemas.

## Open Questions
