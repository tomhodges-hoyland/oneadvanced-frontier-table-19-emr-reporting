# Side-by-Side GP Verification & Digital Sign-Off

## Strategic Context
General Practitioners currently face significant cognitive load and fragmented out-of-hours admin when reviewing third-party medical reports. Validating synthesized answers against multi-page unstructured paper or legacy PDF records requires constant context-switching and increases clinical risk. Delivering a dual-pane verification interface pairs each synthesized response directly with its highlighted EHR source evidence, enabling GPs to clinically verify, adjust redactions, and digitally sign off on reports in 2 to 3 minutes during routine surgery sessions.

## Success Criteria
- Reduction of average GP clinical verification time to under 3 minutes per report.
- 100% compliance with DCB0129 clinical safety standards and Caldicott Guardian verification requirements.
- Sub-500ms synchronized viewport scrolling between report questionnaire fields and linked EHR source evidence.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Dual-pane split-screen UI displaying synthesized report answers alongside highlighted source EHR snippets.
- Interactive spot-editing for questionnaire text, clinical notes, and redaction masks.
- Secretarial rework routing mechanism with contextual annotation flags.
- Cryptographic digital signature stamping and Caldicott approval logging.

### Out of Scope
- Direct patient-facing consultation notes modification during the verification workflow.
- Physical biometric signature capture devices (authenticated via NHS CIS2 session tokens).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a GP selects a synthesized answer field in the review canvas, the Verification Workspace SHALL synchronously scroll the adjacent evidence pane to highlight the exact source EHR encounter snippet.

WHEN a GP completes clinical verification and executes the sign-off action, the Verification Service SHALL apply a cryptographic signature stamp and record an immutable Caldicott audit log entry.

WHERE a GP identifies missing or incorrect synthesis, the Verification Workspace SHALL provide a "Request Rework" workflow with mandatory revision notes routed back to the administrator.

IF any mandatory question remains unanswered or an unreviewed safeguarding flag persists, the Verification Service SHALL disable the digital sign-off action and display an inline validation warning.

## Business Rules
- Only authenticated clinicians with GP Partner or Salaried GP roles verified via NHS CIS2 may execute the digital sign-off action.
- Digital sign-off seals the document artifact, creating a SHA-256 hash preventing any subsequent modifications without revoking the signature.
- A signed report must automatically transition to "Signed & Held in Escrow" state pending billing verification.

## Dependencies
- NHS CIS2 authentication for GP role verification.
- Synthesized report models from the Template Synthesis engine and Redaction Suite.
- PostgreSQL Row-Level Security for practice multi-tenancy isolation.
