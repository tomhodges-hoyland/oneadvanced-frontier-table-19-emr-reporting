# AI-Assisted Third-Party Redaction Suite

## Strategic Context
Fulfilling Subject Access Requests (SARs) and preparing medical reports under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 requires absolute protection of third-party personal identifiable data. Accidental disclosure of family member details, non-clinical third parties, or sensitive safeguarding markers creates severe regulatory breach liabilities with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Introducing an AI-assisted redaction suite automates entity detection and provides one-click irreversible masking, eliminating manual marker-pen workflows while guaranteeing data protection compliance.

## Success Criteria
- Greater than 98% precision and recall in detecting third-party identifiable names, relations, and safeguarding markers.
- Sub-second interactive redaction toggling and visual confirmation in the reviewer workspace.
- 100% irreversible character-level and coordinate-level masking in exported document artifacts.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Clinical NLP entity recognition pipeline targeting third-party names, non-clinical personnel, and safeguarding classifications.
- Interactive redaction UI chips with one-click masking and unmasking controls for administrative staff.
- Custom dictionary and keyword rule-matching for practice-specific safeguarding terms.
- Cryptographic stripping of redacted text from rendered PDF/A documents and JSON exports.

### Out of Scope
- Fully autonomous unreviewed redaction stripping without human administrative/clinical verification.
- Redaction of primary patient health data that forms the core subject of the authorized report.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN clinical narratives are processed through the NLP pipeline, the Redaction Engine SHALL identify and tag third-party individuals, familial relationships, and sensitive safeguarding markers with visual classification chips.

WHEN an administrator clicks a tagged entity chip, the Redaction Engine SHALL toggle the state between visible text and a permanent `[REDACTED]` mask.

WHERE manual redaction is required on unflagged text, the Redaction Engine SHALL support user-selected bounding-box and text-highlight masking.

IF a report draft contains unreviewed high-confidence safeguarding flags, the Redaction Engine SHALL block workflow advancement until all flags are confirmed or dismissed.

## Business Rules
- Redacted text must be permanently removed from underlying document byte streams and PDF object streams, not merely concealed with visual overlays.
- Every redaction action (auto-flagged, confirmed, dismissed, manually added) must generate an immutable Caldicott audit log entry recording user ID and timestamp.
- Third-party clinical staff (e.g. referring GMC-registered doctors) must be treated in accordance with UK GDPR professional exemption rules.

## Dependencies
- Hugging Face / BioBERT clinical NLP models running in UK sovereign AWS ECS Fargate containers.
- Caldicott audit logging database table with Row-Level Security.
