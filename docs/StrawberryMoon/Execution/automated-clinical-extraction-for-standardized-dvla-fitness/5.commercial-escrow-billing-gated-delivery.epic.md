# Commercial Escrow Billing & Gated Delivery

## Strategic Context
UK GP practices lose substantial non-GMS revenue annually due to uncollected, delayed, or untracked fees for commercial medical reports requested by insurers, solicitors, and employers. Traditional workflows release completed reports before invoice settlement, leaving practice managers to pursue unpaid commercial debts. Implementing an integrated payment escrow engine automatically generates statutory and private invoices, withholds finalized documents behind cryptographic access locks, and releases reports instantly upon verified payment settlement, ensuring 100% revenue capture.

## Success Criteria
- 100% capture of private and commercial medical reporting fees prior to document release.
- Sub-2-second automated cryptographic document release following payment gateway webhook confirmation.
- Elimination of manual accounts receivable tracking for third-party commercial medical reports.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Automated invoice generation with practice-configured fee schedules and VAT calculations.
- Integrated payment gateway processing supporting credit card, direct debit, and BACS (Stripe / GoCardless).
- Cryptographic escrow lock engine withholding report downloads until webhook settlement confirmation.
- Secure tokenized download portal with magic-link authentication for external commercial requestors.

### Out of Scope
- Debt collection agency management workflows for disputed historical invoices.
- Invoicing for statutory Subject Access Requests (SARs), which must remain free of charge under UK GDPR.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a GP signs off a commercial medical report, the Billing Gateway SHALL generate a statutory invoice and dispatch a secure payment link to the requesting organization via email.

WHEN the payment gateway emits a verified settlement webhook, the Escrow Engine SHALL unlock the cryptographic release token and transition the request state to "Paid & Dispatched".

WHERE an inquiry is classified as a Subject Access Request (SAR), the Escrow Engine SHALL bypass fee requirements and provide immediate gated delivery in compliance with UK GDPR.

IF a payment webhook verification fails or encounters a signature mismatch, the Escrow Engine SHALL withhold report release, flag an alert in the finance ledger, and notify the practice manager.

## Business Rules
- No commercial medical report may be decrypted or downloaded by a third-party requestor until the payment gateway confirms full settlement.
- Subject Access Requests (SARs) must never be subjected to payment gating under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 rules.
- Commercial requestor download links must utilize time-limited cryptographic tokens expiring after 14 days, with one-click practice re-issuance.

## Dependencies
- Stripe and GoCardless payment gateway webhooks.
- NHS Notify / SendGrid transactional messaging services.
- Sealed report artifacts from the GP Verification Service.
