# Integrated fee escrow and automated billing engine ensuring

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** EMR Reporting
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
EMR Reporting Launches Integrated Fee Escrow and Automated Billing Engine to Guarantee 100% Private Report Fee Collection

## Summary
EMR Reporting introduces an integrated fee escrow and automated billing engine designed specifically for GP practices managing private medical reports. This release ensures practices collect 100% of non-GMS private report fees before releasing documents, eliminating revenue leakage and streamlining cash flow for private report services.

## Problem Statement
GP practices managing private medical reports face significant revenue challenges: fees are often uncollected before documents are released, creating cash flow gaps and administrative burden. Manual billing workflows lead to missed payments, incomplete invoicing, and delayed or absent fee recovery. Practices currently lack a system to guarantee fee collection before report delivery, resulting in lost income and time spent on follow-up and collections.

## Solution
EMR Reporting's integrated fee escrow and automated billing engine holds private report documents in escrow until fees are collected and confirmed. The system automates billing workflows, tracks payment status in real time, and releases documents only when full payment is received. This guarantees 100% fee collection upfront and eliminates the administrative overhead of manual collections management.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Fee escrow on private reports | Guarantees payment before document release | 100% fee collection rate |
| Automated billing engine | Eliminates manual invoice creation and tracking | 50% reduction in billing admin time |
| Real-time payment status tracking | Provides complete visibility into outstanding fees | Improved cash flow and forecasting accuracy |
| Document release gate | Links report access to payment confirmation | Zero revenue leakage on private reports |
| Integrated payment reconciliation | Reconciles receipts against invoices automatically | Reduced accounting errors and reconciliation time |

## Market/Competitor Differentiation
EMR Reporting is setting the trend in private report revenue protection. Competitors offer standalone billing tools or basic report management, but none integrate escrow enforcement with automated billing specifically for GP practices. This release positions us as the first solution to guarantee 100% fee collection before document release, directly addressing a pain point competitors have overlooked and delivering measurable financial impact to practices immediately.
