# Launch of automated Subject Access Request (SAR) extraction

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** EMR Reporting
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
EMR Reporting launches automated SAR extraction and redaction to eliminate GP administrative backlogs and ensure UK GDPR compliance

## Summary
EMR Reporting today introduces automated Subject Access Request (SAR) extraction and redaction workflows, enabling General Practice teams to process patient data requests in minutes rather than weeks. The new capability eliminates manual document handling, reduces compliance risk, and frees administrative staff to focus on patient care. UK General Practices can now meet GDPR response deadlines consistently while maintaining data protection standards.

## Problem Statement
General Practices currently face significant administrative burden when handling Subject Access Requests. Manual extraction of patient records from disparate EMR systems, followed by careful redaction of sensitive third-party data, consumes dozens of staff hours per request. This process creates bottlenecks, delays patient responses beyond GDPR's 30-day deadline, and exposes practices to regulatory penalties and reputational damage. Administrative teams struggle to balance request volume with accuracy, often prioritizing speed over thoroughness.

## Solution
EMR Reporting's automated SAR workflow intelligently identifies, extracts, and redacts patient-related documents across the entire EMR in a single operation. Machine learning-powered redaction recognizes third-party clinician notes, references to other patients, and sensitive metadata, removing them automatically while preserving the patient's own clinical information. Practice staff simply authorize the request, and the system delivers a compliant, audit-ready PDF within minutes. Built-in compliance tracking ensures every request meets UK GDPR requirements and creates a permanent record for regulatory inspection.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|

| Audit trail and compliance tracking | Every request logged with timestamps, approvals, and redaction details | Full accountability for inspections; defensible response documentation |

## Market/Competitor Differentiation
EMR Reporting is the first practice management solution to combine intelligent SAR automation with third-party redaction in a single workflow, setting a new standard for GDPR compliance in UK General Practice. Competitors require manual processes or offer only extraction tools, leaving redaction to staff—a time-consuming, error-prone step. By automating the entire SAR lifecycle end-to-end, EMR Reporting eliminates the compliance-speed tradeoff and establishes itself as the gold standard for data protection in primary care.
