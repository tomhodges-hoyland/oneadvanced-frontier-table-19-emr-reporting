# Next-generation contextual AI redaction engine specifically trained on complex UK primary care notes to automatically scrub third-party identifiers and sensitive non-disclosable clinical history

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** EMR Reporting
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
EMR Reporting Introduces Contextual AI Redaction Engine for UK Primary Care – Automatically Identifies and Removes Sensitive Identifiers and Non-Disclosable Clinical History

## Summary
EMR Reporting launches a next-generation contextual AI redaction engine purpose-built for complex UK primary care notes. The engine automatically detects and redacts third-party identifiers, sensitive non-disclosable clinical history, and other restricted content—eliminating manual review overhead and ensuring compliance with UK data governance requirements. This release transforms how primary care organizations handle patient record sharing and disclosure requests.

## Problem Statement
UK primary care practices face mounting pressure to safely share patient records across care settings while protecting sensitive information and maintaining confidentiality. Manual redaction is labor-intensive, error-prone, and inconsistent. Clinical staff must navigate complex rules around what constitutes "non-disclosable" clinical history—including references to third-party patients, protective notes, and historical trauma—while ensuring no sensitive identifiers slip through. This manual process consumes significant resources and creates compliance risk.

## Solution
The EMR Reporting contextual AI redaction engine uses advanced natural language understanding trained on thousands of UK primary care notes to automatically identify and redact sensitive content in real time. The engine understands clinical context—distinguishing between clinically relevant information and non-disclosable references—and applies UK-specific governance rules. Organizations can now share records confidently with minimal manual intervention, accelerating disclosure requests while maintaining rigorous data protection standards.
