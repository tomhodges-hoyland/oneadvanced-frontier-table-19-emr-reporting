# Market Structure

## Overview 
**Confidence Level**: 85%

This market segment comprises Primary Care General Practice (GP) surgeries and Primary Care Networks (PCNs) in the United Kingdom seeking automated software solutions to streamline, collate, and redact electronic medical record (EMR/EHR) data for third-party non-NHS medical reports. Clinicians and practice administrative staff currently spend significant, non-reimbursed or low-margin clinical hours manually sifting through patient record systems (such as EMIS Web, TPP SystmOne, and Vision) to answer structured medical reporting requests from insurance underwriters, legal firms, Subject Access Requests (SARs), the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA), and employers (occupational fitness-to-work evaluations). Automating information extraction, chronological summarization, and redaction relieves severe administrative bottlenecks, enhances non-GMS (General Medical Services) revenue capture, and mitigates regulatory risk under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

## Customer Needs Analysis

### Functional Requirements
- Seamless, secure API/EHR integration with EMIS Web, TPP SystmOne, and Microtest/Vision
- Automated structured data extraction for standard reporting templates (DVLA medical questionnaires, ABI/insurance reports, Department for Work and Pensions forms, Subject Access Requests)
- Robust automated third-party data detection, sensitive record flagging, and one-click redaction suite compliant with UK GDPR
- Direct digital report delivery and invoicing engine to ensure practice fee collection from requesting corporate entities prior to release
- Audit trail and digital signature support for supervising GP review and sign-off within 2–3 minutes

### Key Pain Points
- Severe clinical burnout: General Practitioners spending 4 to 8 hours weekly outside consultation hours manually searching medical histories for non-NHS administrative reporting
- Financial leakage: Hundreds of uncollected or delayed private report fees due to manual invoicing and untracked report completion cycles
- Turnaround compliance risk: Failing to meet statutory 30-day Subject Access Request (SAR) deadlines, risking Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) regulatory breach
- High administrative burden on secretarial teams: Endless back-and-forth scanning, printing, redaction with black marker pens, and manual collation of paper and legacy PDF files

## Competitive Landscape

### Top Competitors
- **iGPR (Niche Health / Clanwilliam Group)**: Longstanding market incumbent providing electronic GP reporting (eGPR) and automated redaction integrated with EMIS Web and SystmOne, heavily adopted across UK general practice for insurer and legal reports.
- **Medi2data (eMR)**: Leading dedicated medical reporting platform in the UK, specializing in digital report extraction, insurer connectivity, and workflow automation for GP surgeries.
- **Docman / Advanced Health & Care**: Dominant primary care document workflow platform across NHS general practice, offering record transfer, workflow management, and digital document processing.
- **Manual / In-House EHR Search Tools**: Native search and query tools within EMIS Web and SystmOne combined with standalone PDF redaction tools (e.g., Adobe Acrobat, e-Redact).
